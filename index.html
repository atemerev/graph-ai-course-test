About Me

Researcher and engineer at the intersection of agency, memory, and cognition — both biological and artificial.

I'm Alexander Temerev. I built Lethe (lethe.gg), an open-source autonomous AI agent whose architecture maps functional neuroanatomy onto a multi-actor system: persistent memory via autoassociative recall, salience-gated attention, and background cognition.

Previously I worked on biologically detailed whole-brain simulations at EPFL Blue Brain Project, developed geospatial agent-based epidemic models at the University of Geneva, and ran multi-agent simulations with emergent dynamics at CERN.

My background also includes 13 years building low-latency trading systems and quantitative simulation platforms across the Swiss financial industry.

Curriculum

About the Course

An 8-week guided bootcamp covering no-code AI tools for responsible task automation. Weekly live workshops, personalized feedback, and a capstone project.

Module 1

Websites, Documents & Presentations

Learn how LLMs work. Generate text for papers or grants, create project websites with no-code tools, and design presentations using AI assistants.

Module 2

AI-Automated Data Analysis

Master AI tools for analyzing data, manipulating files, and creating automated reports. Build dashboards and design spreadsheet automations.

Module 3

GenAI for Research & Text Analysis

AI techniques for literature search, text classification, sentiment analysis, and knowledge synthesis from large document collections.

Module 4

Custom GPTs & Chatbots

Build chatbots for research assistance and knowledge management. Explore RAG systems, API integrations, and Botpress workflows.

Module 5

AI for Task Automation

Design responsible AI agents for literature monitoring, email response automations, and browser control for web-based research tasks.

Module 6

GenAI for Communication & Outreach

AI-driven research dissemination: summaries for different audiences, scientific figures, video abstracts, and content scheduling.

Module 7

Capstone Project & Showcase

Collaborate with peers to create a final AI solution. Present your project to an audience of peers and guests, demonstrating responsible AI practice.
